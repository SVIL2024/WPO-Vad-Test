==============================================================================
TAKEAWAY
==============================================================================
  Across every measurement we have, 4-5 seeds resolve roughly
  1.51 to 7.18 absolute points (AP) at 80% power.
  Best case  : XD AP, official 5 seeds, col1      -> 1.51 pp
  Worst case : our E2 AP, 4 seeds, 1 epoch        -> 7.18 pp
  Typical published VAD gains: 0.5-2 pp -> BELOW the resolution
  in every configuration we measured.

  Caveat to state in the paper: these use sigma_d = sd as an UPPER
  bound where no paired sd was measured; successful pairing (section
  3.3) shrinks sigma_d by ~2.4x and improves the MDE accordingly.
