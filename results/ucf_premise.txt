UCF-Crime premise test -- the band defined in seconds, not frames
==============================================================================

why: XD features are at the native 15 fps (a 240 s window holds 3600
rows); UCF features are one sampled clip per video at ~30 fps. The
paper's mid band is bins 3-5 of a 64-point transform = 10-21 frames =
0.67-1.40 s. On UCF those same bins cover 0.33-0.70 s, so the two
datasets are compared at different periods unless the bins move.

scope: UCF features are pooled 512-d vectors only, with no patch
tokens, so this is the GLOBAL view. The paper's tab:cluster reports
the Ped2 global view as the one whose 95% CI covers zero, so a null
here is consistent with that and would not refute the premise.
