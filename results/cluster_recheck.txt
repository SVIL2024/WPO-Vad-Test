READING
==========================================================================
  'published' = the window-level z that is in the paper today.
  'cluster bootstrap' = the same statistic with the VIDEOS, not the
  overlapping windows, as the sampling units.
  'VIDEO-level paired contrast' = one number per video, paired within
  video: the conservative re-analysis, and the one the paper should
  quote when the videos are the independent units.
  Every view above reproduced its published window-level z first; the
  'after' numbers cannot come from a different statistic.
