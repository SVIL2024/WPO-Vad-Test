CONCLUSION: the reference does NOT peak at epoch 1 in these runs
(3 of 5 UCF seeds peak at epoch 3).  The paper must therefore not
claim that a 1-epoch budget is like-for-like with the reference; it is
a shorter budget, and the correct framing is that our noise figures are
measured at the shorter end while both budgets are reported.
