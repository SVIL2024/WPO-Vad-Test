  READING
    The DIRECTION is consistent -- 3 of 4 seeds put the wave field
    below control, and the one seed that reverses it does so by more than
    any of the other three differences. What does not survive is the
    MAGNITUDE: the published single-seed figure of -0.0754 is about 7.5x the
    paired estimate, and the paired estimate is not distinguishable from
    zero.
    So the largest effect in tab:mechanisms does not hold up at the
    magnitude reported. That is this paper's own thesis applied to the
    paper's own strongest row: a single-seed screen at these effect
    sizes cannot distinguish a cost of about one AP point from a seed
    draw, and -0.0754 is inside the noise of the very table that reports it.
TWO CONFOUNDS WE HAVE NOT EXCLUDED, so this is not a refutation
  1. Batch size. src/xd_option.py defaults to 96; this ran at 64. Both
     arms are 0.0313 below the published control, which is why the
     absolute levels are not comparable and only the contrast is quoted.
     A wave-field/batch-size interaction is not ruled out.
  2. Fusion wiring. This ran --wave-fusion logits, which is the option
     default, but the published row carries no fusion label while two
     separate rows in the same table are labelled evidence->logits1 and
     feature gate. If the published row used the feature wiring, this is
     a different branch.
