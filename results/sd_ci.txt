(2) THE COMPARATIVE CLAIM: AP vs AUC, same seeds
    AP and AUC come from the same runs, so the ratio is resampled PAIRED
    (all 5^5 resamples enumerated; no Monte Carlo error).

    dataset         point      95% CI on ratio P(ratio<1) P(ratio<2)
    UCF-Crime        4.2x  [  1.5x,   6.0x]       0.003      0.080
               (sd AUC 0.0064, sd AP 0.0168, n = 5 seeds, 3120 resamples)
    XD-Violence      3.0x  [  1.3x,   4.3x]       0.000      0.179
               (sd AUC 0.0023, sd AP 0.0091, n = 5 seeds, 3120 resamples)

    Read: the DIRECTION is robust -- the probability that AP needs no more
    seeds than AUC is 0.003 (UCF) and 0.000 (XD). The MAGNITUDE is soft:
    the paper's 'three to seven times' should be read as a point estimate
    whose interval runs from about 1.3x to 6x.
