READING
  The residual AUCs are identical by construction -- only the standard
  error moves, and it moves by the SE ratio, so the published z
  is inflated by that factor.  The paper's claim about this table is
  the ORDERING (the magnitude control leads after residualising, and
  the strongest oscillatory variant loses to it), not the magnitude of
  any z; the ordering is a property of the point estimates and so is
  untouched by the unit.
  Largest SE ratio: waveD_fullD, z +16.19 -> +1.95 (8.3x, i.e. deff 69x).
  SE ratio range 7.6-8.3x  ->  deff range 57-69x.
  Variants that keep z > 3 at the video unit: ut_magnitude, wave_c0.
